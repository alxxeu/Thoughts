Capture a thought right from the notch, keep deleted cards safe in the new Archive, and make your Space bar your own. Thoughts 1.5 is here.

## What’s New in This Version (≤ 4000 characters, 1289)

Thoughts 1.5

NEW
• Archive — a deleted card (hold the ×, or Clear Space) waits in Settings → Archive for 30 days. Restore it to its Space or delete it for good.
• Quick Capture grows out of the notch — a glass panel with the Space you’re saving to; press ⌥↑ or ⌥↓ to pick another one.
• Space bar — swipe it with two fingers or drag to jump to the next Space. It also shows over a locked Space and on your desktop.
• Backgrounds — Material, colors that adapt to light and dark, or your own image, the same in a window, full screen and on the desktop.
• Focus Mode with a larger glass card, an exit arrow and a pinch to focus.
• Kanban — a + in every column header, Return adds a tile above, done tiles sink.
• View → Customize Space Bar, next to Desktop Overlay.

IMPROVED
• Tidy Cards evens out card sizes in each corner so they stack into neat blocks.
• Entering and leaving the desktop fades instead of jumping.
• A new animation for Spoiler and Lock: the stars flow across neighboring cards.
• Cards grow and shrink around their own center.
• ⌥Tab shows the Spaces three in a row.

FIXED
• Dividers keep their dashes after you change the text color.
• A stale ⌘F no longer lingers after leaving Focus Mode.
• Checklist turns a bare [x] line into a proper task instead of removing it.
