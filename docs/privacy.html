Privacy Policy

Thoughts for macOS · Effective September 10, 2026 · Updated September 20, 2026

1. Overview

Thoughts, by itself, does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, from you or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. The one exception is the optional AI features described in Section 4: if you turn one on and supply your own API key, the text you act on is sent directly to that provider. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly enable that yourself.

2. Where your data lives

Everything you create in Thoughts — cards, text, tags, Space names — is stored locally, inside the app's own sandboxed container on your Mac. Nothing is ever uploaded to a server, because Thoughts doesn't have one.

3. Passcode & Touch ID

Your passcode is stored in the macOS Keychain, on your device only, using Apple's own security frameworks. Touch ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework and never leaves your Mac.

4. AI features

Thoughts can optionally use AI to summarize or rewrite a card, or to answer questions about a Space. These actions are off by default and only run if you turn one on yourself in Settings.

Apple Intelligence runs entirely on your Mac (macOS 26 or later), the same as the rest of Thoughts. No text ever leaves your device for this option, and no API key is required.

ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic) are bring-your-own-key: you supply your own API key for the provider you choose, and it's stored in the macOS Keychain on your device, the same way as your passcode. When you use one of these providers, the text of the card you're acting on — or, for Space-level actions like Ask AI, the text of that Space's non-private cards — is sent directly from your Mac to OpenAI's or Anthropic's API, using your own key. That request goes straight from your Mac to the provider; it is never routed through a server operated by the developer, because there isn't one. Cards or Spaces you've locked or marked private are automatically excluded from Space-level AI context, the same as Spotlight search below.

What OpenAI or Anthropic do with the text you send them is governed by their own privacy policies (OpenAI, Anthropic), not this one.

5. Spotlight search

Thoughts indexes your card text locally so macOS Spotlight can search it. This index lives only on your device and is managed by macOS itself. Cards or Spaces you've locked or marked private are automatically excluded from this index.

6. Children's privacy

Thoughts is not directed at children and collects no data from any user, regardless of age.
