Privacy Policy

Thoughts for macOS · Effective September 10, 2026 · Updated September 29, 2026

1. Overview

Thoughts, by itself, does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data, from you or anyone else. There is no account, no analytics, and no third-party SDKs of any kind. There are two optional exceptions, both off by default: iCloud sync (Section 3), which stores your data in your own iCloud account, end-to-end encrypted; and the AI features (Section 5), which send the text you act on directly to the provider you choose. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you explicitly enable that yourself.

2. Where your data lives

Everything you create in Thoughts — cards, text, tags, Space names — is stored locally, inside the app's own sandboxed container on your Mac. The developer never receives any of it, because Thoughts doesn't have a server.

3. iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on Sync with iCloud in Settings, your Spaces and cards — text, formatting, tags, positions and privacy settings — are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other Macs. The content of every card and Space is end-to-end encrypted with keys kept in your iCloud Keychain: neither Apple nor the developer can read it. Apple only sees technical metadata such as how many records exist and when they changed.

With sync on, your Space Lock passcode and your AI API keys (if you've added any) are also synced through iCloud Keychain, which is end-to-end encrypted as well. Appearance settings stay separate on each Mac. Turning sync off keeps everything on your Mac; Delete iCloud Data removes your Thoughts data from iCloud.

4. Passcode & Touch ID

Your passcode is stored in the macOS Keychain using Apple's own security frameworks — on your device only, or, with iCloud sync on, also in your iCloud Keychain (see Section 3). Touch ID authentication is handled entirely by Apple's LocalAuthentication framework and never leaves your Mac.

5. AI features

Thoughts can optionally use AI to summarize or rewrite a card, or to answer questions about a Space. These actions are off by default and only run if you turn one on yourself in Settings.

Apple Intelligence runs entirely on your Mac (macOS 26 or later), the same as the rest of Thoughts. No text ever leaves your device for this option, and no API key is required.

ChatGPT (OpenAI) and Claude (Anthropic) are bring-your-own-key: you supply your own API key for the provider you choose, and it's stored in the macOS Keychain, the same way as your passcode (synced through your iCloud Keychain only if iCloud sync is on). When you use one of these providers, the text of the card you're acting on — or, for Space-level actions like Ask AI, the text of that Space's non-private cards — is sent directly from your Mac to OpenAI's or Anthropic's API, using your own key. That request goes straight from your Mac to the provider; it is never routed through a server operated by the developer, because there isn't one. Cards or Spaces you've locked or marked private are automatically excluded from Space-level AI context, the same as Spotlight search below.

What OpenAI or Anthropic do with the text you send them is governed by their own privacy policies (OpenAI, Anthropic), not this one.

6. Backups & export

Backups and Markdown exports are files you create yourself and save wherever you choose; Thoughts doesn't send them anywhere. A backup can be encrypted with a password you set (AES-256). Automatic safety copies made before an import or before the first iCloud sync stay inside the app's container on your Mac.

7. Spotlight search

Thoughts indexes your card text locally so macOS Spotlight can search it. This index lives only on your device and is managed by macOS itself. Cards or Spaces you've locked or marked private are automatically excluded from this index.

8. Children's privacy

Thoughts is not directed at children and collects no data from any user, regardless of age.
